The reference shelf

Every explanation points to one of these books. They are the standard references behind the questions, from the classical heritage to today's textbooks.

The classical heritage

  • De Materia MedicaDioscorides, 1st century AD
  • al-Ḥāwī (Liber Continens)Al-Razi, 10th century
  • Zād al-Musāfir (Viaticum)Ibn al-Jazzar of Kairouan, 10th century
  • The Canon of MedicineIbn Sina, early 11th century
  • Compendium on Simple Medicaments and FoodsIbn al-Bayṭār, 13th century
  • An Account of the FoxgloveWilliam Withering, 1785

Pharmacology

  • The Pharmacological Basis of TherapeuticsGoodman & Gilman, first published 1941
  • Rang & Dale's PharmacologyCore text for medical and pharmacy students
  • Basic & Clinical PharmacologyKatzung

Medicine

  • The Principles and Practice of MedicineWilliam Osler, 1892
  • Harrison's Principles of Internal MedicineFirst edited by Tinsley R. Harrison, 1950
  • Davidson's Principles and Practice of MedicineStandard text in many medical schools

Pharmaceutics and practice

  • Remington: The Science and Practice of PharmacyFounded by Joseph P. Remington
  • Martindale: The Complete Drug ReferenceFounded by William Martindale, 1883
  • Aulton's PharmaceuticsThe Design and Manufacture of Medicines
  • Stockley's Drug InteractionsThe reference for interaction checks
  • British National Formulary (BNF)Prescribing and counselling reference

Industry and clinical trials

  • Fundamentals of Clinical TrialsFriedman, Furberg & DeMets
  • The Theory and Practice of Industrial PharmacyLieberman & Lachman
